The only thing that eats Ivo's lock is three measures of grog, and the cups carrying it are eating themselves. Get it to the lock before there is none left.

The cups

Every vessel carries a volume and an exposure deadline, and each fails in stages on its own clock. A cup that leaves the barrel starts counting; pouring into a fresh cup restarts its own exposure, and pouring never moves the damage. An empty cup stops degrading, which is the only reason a relay works at all.

Walkthrough

  1. Press Pour for the lock at once. The cups are already counting.
  2. Mara walks to the lock pouring A to B and B to C before each cup gives out.
  3. Four measures must reach the lock to dissolve it. Miss a pour and the cup is gone; press Restart and try again sooner.
