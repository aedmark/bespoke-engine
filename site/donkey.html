THE DONKEY OF LAMP POINT

The wreckers' old chart is in the cellar under the lighthouse on Lamp Point. Between Tamsin and the hatch stands Cudgel, the keeper's donkey, on a long rope, and he has decided he hates her.

Play

You are Tamsin. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark (the round markers on the ground); each is named on the stage. Tamsin walks over before she acts; Look works from where she stands. Point before you click: what a click would act on lights up with its action, such as [Use Old Anchor], and the line above the verbs says it too.

Cudgel

Cudgel is tied to the Mooring Post, and his rope reaches the Cellar Hatch. Go near the hatch and he shoves you flat, then ambles back to his post. Watch the label under him: while it says he is ambling back, you have a little while before he turns round.

Rooms

Two rooms: Lamp Point, the headland under the ruined lighthouse, and the Wreckers Cellar below it. Once the way is clear, Use the Cellar Hatch to go down; walk onto Cellar Steps to come back up.

Walkthrough

Show the solution
  1. Use the Cellar Hatch. Cudgel shoves Tamsin back, and heads for his post.
  2. While he ambles back, Use the Old Anchor: Tamsin heaves it into the line of his rope.
  3. Use the Cellar Hatch again. With his rope caught on the anchor, he can't reach it, and down she goes.
  4. Look at the Wreckers Chart.
