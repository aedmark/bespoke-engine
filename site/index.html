The Architectural Pillars

Why BeSpoke Flips the Paradigm

Modern game development drowns in pointer spaghetti, OOP inheritance, and frame-rate delta accumulation. BeSpoke reclaims the craft of simulation.

🏛️

Engine-as-Spine

The VM is main(). It owns the master discrete tick clock, the entity IDs, spatial topologies, and relational facts. Visuals (SDL2, Raylib, Sokol, WebCanvas) are merely downstream, stateless peripheral display bridges.

// Core simulation is zero-dependency Zig
var world = World.init(allocator);
var vm = VM.init(&world, &chunk);
🗄️

Flat Relational State

No class hierarchies. No pointers inside serialized state. Truth consists of contiguous 4-tuples: (Subject, Relation, Object, TTL). Built-in TTL decay allows charms, distractions, and buffs to expire naturally without cleanup spaghetti.

remember Carries(Guard, Cheese)
remember CharmedBy(Guard, Bard) for 180 ticks
⏳

Fibers & Time Travel

Write game logic like a play script using cooperative fibers with built-in wait and wait until. Because state is a flat contiguous array, snapshots are a single memcpy. Rewind time instantly!

walk Guard to Cellar
wait 120 ticks
walk Guard to Hearth

The Three Verified Scenarios

Every engine claims to be elegant in theory. BeSpoke proves its invariants across three classic, systemically distinct adventure game vertical slices.

Vertical Slice 01 · Systemic Emergence

The Tavern Heist

A classic systemic stealth scenario. A patrolling Guard holds delicious Cheese. A Bard plays an enchanting melody on his lute, charming the guard. A sneaky Cat seizes the opportunity to swipe the cheese right under the guard's charmed nose!

Priority Rule Preemption TTL Decay Dice Rolls A* Graph Navigation 
entity Guard, Bard, Cat, Cheese
relation Carries(holder, item)
relation CharmedBy(listener, musician)

routine Patrol(Guard):
  loop:
    walk Guard to Cellar
    wait 120 ticks
    walk Guard to Hearth
    wait 120 ticks

on cue PlayLute(Bard, target):
  if roll 2d6 >= 8:
    remember CharmedBy(target, Bard) for 180 ticks

on cue Steal(Cat, victim) where CharmedBy(victim, _) and Carries(victim, loot):
  forget Carries(victim, loot)
  remember Carries(Cat, loot)

Booty Island Spitting Contest

An homage to Monkey Island 2. Guybrush must win the championship spit. The crowd and judge watch closely. Guybrush sounds the ship's horn to distract them, tampers with the distance flags, downs a thick green drink, and waits for a gust of wind to blow toward the target!

Vertical Slice 03 · Multi-Scene & Crafting

Gabriel Knight 3: The Cat Moustache

Jane Jensen's infamous 1999 puzzle recreated with systemic dignity. Gabriel must rent Mosely's motorcycle from Madame Buthane. He scavenges tape, marker, and syrup from his desk, snatches Mosely's passport, tapes a garden fence hole, squirts the cat to harvest fur, crafts a cat-hair moustache with syrup, and fools Buthane!
