THE BELL TOWER

Maren has a staff that plays what it hears. Four notes are a tune, a tune is a change, and a tune played backwards undoes itself.

Play

You are Maren. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark (the round markers on the ground); she walks over before she acts. Look works from where she stands. Point before you click: what a click would act on lights up with its action.

Drafts

A draft is four notes. Act on the thing you mean to change, so the staff has a target, then play the notes on the keys below. The staff's reach starts at four notes, which is one tune, and grows when you find something that widens it. Each draft also has a reverse: play it backwards and it does the opposite, so a binding tune loosens and a kindling tune douses.

Walkthrough

  1. Look at the lantern: its flame spells C D E C, which is Kindle.
  2. Play C D E C with the brazier as the target to light it, then use the stairwell.
  3. Look at the ivy: F E D F is Bind. In the belfry, play it backwards, F D E F, on the iron chain to free it.
  4. Take the tuning fork to reach all seven notes, then play G A B G on the great bell.

Keys

  • C, D, E, F, G, A, B: play a note
  • S: play the next step for you
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • Right-click: next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
