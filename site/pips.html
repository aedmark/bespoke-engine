THE PIP-SPITTING GREEN

The harbour fair's pip-spitting contest, and Tamsin means to win it. Judge Marrow keeps a sharp eye on his pegs, and the sea breeze comes and goes.

Play

You are Tamsin. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark (the round markers on the ground); each is named on the stage. Tamsin walks over before she acts; Look works from where she stands. Point before you click: what a click would act on lights up with its action, such as [Talk to Judge Marrow], and the line above the verbs says it too.

Items

What Tamsin carries sits in the tray after ITEMS, to the right of the verbs. Click an item to use it. An item that needs something to work on is taken in hand instead: then click what to use it on, whether a person, a prop, another item, or Tamsin herself. Right-click puts it away.

Spit

Press Spit (or P) to spit from where you stand, or talk to Judge Marrow to spit or hear the rules. Spit past the last peg to take first place. The breeze comes about every 45 seconds and blows for 20; spit when the narrator says it has picked up. A distraction lasts 45 seconds.

Walkthrough

Show the solution
  1. Click the Treacle Cider in the tray: Tamsin drinks it, and her spit turns thick.
  2. Click the Conch: the judge and the crowd turn to look at the harbour.
  3. While they look away, Use the Pegs to move them.
  4. Wait for the sea breeze.
  5. When it picks up, press Spit.

Keys

  • 1 to 9: pick a line
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • P: spit
  • S: play the next step for you
  • Right-click: put an item away, or next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
