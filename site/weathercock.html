THE COPPER WEATHERCOCK

At midnight the copper weathercock from the Customs House roof comes through Wren Halloway's attic. The Customs House still has its own: this one is from 28 days ahead, the night the autumn gale tears it loose. Brackwater is living in a borrowed month, and the month is running out.

Play

You are Wren. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark; each is named on the stage. Look works from where you stand; other verbs walk over first. Walk to the attic stairs to go down to the Customs House, and to the hill road at its far end to climb Beacon Hill.

The borrowed month

Everything you do happens in a copy of the world with a countdown at the top of the stage. Close the loop before it reaches zero and the copy folds back into the real Brackwater, keeping what you did. Let it run out and the month tears away; rewind with the timeline and try again.

Items

What Wren carries shows in the tray after ITEMS. Click an item to take it in hand, then click a person or a prop to use it there. Right-click puts it away.

Walkthrough

Show the solution
  1. Talk to Pilot Ashe if you want the countdown from him.
  2. Take the weathercock, then walk to the attic stairs.
  3. In the Customs House, take the sluice crank and use it on the sluice.
  4. Walk to the hill road at the far end.
  5. On Beacon Hill, take the note from Hester Quill's post box, then talk to her and give it to her.
  6. Use the weathercock on the fog whirl.

Keys

  • 1 to 9: pick a line
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • S: play the next step for you
  • Right-click: put an item away, or next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
