THE WELL OF THREE TIDES

Doctor Brine's Tide Wells join three centuries of Brackwater. Ossie is in 1712, Nell in 1912, and Juno is stuck up an orange tree in 2112, in a town run by gulls. Whatever goes down one well comes up in another century's bucket.

Play

You start as Nell. The buttons under the stage switch between Ossie, Nell, and Juno, each in their own century. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark; each is named on the stage. Look works from where you stand; other verbs walk over first.

The wells

Each player carries things in the tray after ITEMS. Click an item to take it in hand, then click your Tide Well to drop it down the shaft. From Nell's well in 1912 it comes up in Ossie's bucket in 1712. A cornerstone box sealed in 1712 is still sealed in 1912, with whatever was put inside two hundred years older.

Cause and effect

What happens in 1712 is history by 2112. Change the past and look at the future: a tree that falls in Ossie's time is gone in Juno's, and a banner sewn in 1712 flies over the Gull Roost.

Walkthrough

Show the solution
  1. As Nell, use the Cheap Port on the Tide Well.
  2. As Ossie, use the Cheap Port on the Cornerstone Box.
  3. As Nell, use the Cornerstone Box: the port is vinegar now. Use the Aged Vinegar, then the Gull Handbill, on the Tide Well.
  4. As Ossie, use the Aged Vinegar on the Voltaic Jar, and the Charged Jar on the Tide Well.
  5. Use the Gull Handbill on Widow Fenn, then Talk to her and give her the new design.
  6. Use the Red Lead Paint on the Orange Tree, then Talk to Founder Gale and warn him of the red blight.
  7. As Juno, now on the roof, Talk to Admiral Squall and ask to feed the gulls.
  8. Use the Gull Banner on the roof to take it, then use the Gull Banner on Juno.

Keys

  • 1 to 9: pick a line
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • S: play the next step for you
  • Right-click: put an item away, or next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
