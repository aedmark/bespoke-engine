THE SALTMARKET

Pilot Ashe drowned forty years ago, and his logbook went missing with him. It has turned up in Brackwater's Saltmarket, tucked behind Broker Quince's bills of lading. Tamsin wants it, and there are three ways in.

Play

You are Tamsin. First choose how to go about it: click a line, or press 1, 2, or 3. Then pick a verb and click a person, a prop, or a mark; each is named on the stage. Look works from where you stand; other verbs walk over first.

Three ways in

Cunning reads Quince's tally book, Muscle goes past his porter, and Crew brings in the net-menders. Whichever you choose shuts the other two gates, so Tamsin walks through the one left open, and Quince only talks about the way you chose. Restart or rewind to try another.

Walkthrough

Show the solution
  1. Choose a way in: Cunning, Muscle, or Crew.
  2. Talk to Broker Quince if you want his side of it.
  3. Take the logbook. Tamsin walks there through the open gate and gets it her chosen way.

Keys

  • 1 to 9: pick a line
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • S: play the next step for you
  • R: rewind
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
