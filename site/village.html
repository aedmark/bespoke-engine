THE LIVING VILLAGE

Six villagers run their own day on hunger, energy, and money. Then a drifter arrives with nothing, and the settlement does the rest.

Watch

The clock runs by itself, a tick a second. Nobody is scripted to steal, talk, or arrest: the Sheriff, Baker, Farmer, Innkeeper, and Gossip each answer their own needs, and what happens follows from who was where and who saw it. Look at the labels under the people for what they are doing.

What to watch

Hunger rises and energy falls, so villagers visit the tavern, sleep in their cottages, and work their marks. A need they cannot meet turns into a stand-in that draws money and food. When something is taken in front of a witness, the witness speaks, a rumour travels, and the Sheriff acts on it.

Make it happen

Press Stir the village: the Drifter is made starving and penniless and walks to the bakery. The rest is emergence, not staging. Press Restart and let the same village run without that shove to compare.

Keys

  • S: stir the village
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
  • Right-click: nothing here, the village needs no verbs
