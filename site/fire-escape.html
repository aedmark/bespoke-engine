THE CASTLE FIRE ESCAPE

Wren is shut in the long gallery of Hollin Keep. The way out is a door behind the hanging tapestry, and the only light in the room is a wall torch that will happily set the tapestry alight.

Play

You are Wren. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark (the round markers on the ground); she walks over before she acts. Point before you click: what a click would act on lights up with its action. The three buttons under the stage run Wren's actions from wherever she stands.

Fire

The gallery floor is a grid of cells. Flammable cells burn for a fixed time, spread fire to their neighbours, give off smoke, and a burning cell can be quenched by water flowing along the floor. Fire, smoke, and water are facts with lifetimes, so the same layout always burns the same way.

Walkthrough

  1. Fill bucket at the trough.
  2. Light it at the torch. The tapestry burns away and the hidden door shows through.
  3. Wait while the fire reaches the door, then Throw water on it to soak the wood. The water floods back along the corridor and puts the fire out.
  4. Walk out through the door.

Keys

  • S: play the next step for you
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • Right-click: next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
