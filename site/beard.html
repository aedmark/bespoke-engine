THE BORROWED BEARD

Captain Rook holds the last berth on the packet boat, and Tamsin needs it. All it takes is his papers, a beard, and a little nerve.

Play

You are Tamsin. Pick a verb, then click a person, a prop, or a mark (the round markers on the ground); each is named on the stage. Tamsin walks over before she acts; Look works from where she stands. Point before you click: what a click would act on lights up with its action, such as [Talk to Captain Rook], and the line above the verbs says it too.

Items

What Tamsin carries sits in the tray after ITEMS, to the right of the verbs. Click an item to use it. An item that needs something to work on is taken in hand instead: then click what to use it on, whether a person, a prop, another item, or Tamsin herself. Right-click puts it away.

Rooms

Three rooms: the Inn Landing, the Net Loft Yard, and the Harbour Office. Walk onto the Stairs to go down to the yard, Yard Path to go back in, To Harbour Office to cross to the office, and Office Door to come back out.

Walkthrough

Show the solution
  1. Use the Wash Stand: a charcoal stick, sail tape, and a honey pot.
  2. Wait for Captain Rook to walk off down the landing, then Use Rook Door to borrow his papers.
  3. Use the Charcoal on the Rook Papers to give his portrait a beard.
  4. Walk to the Stairs, down to the Net Loft Yard.
  5. Use the Rain Barrel: a watering can.
  6. Use the Sail Tape on the Gate Gap.
  7. Use the Watering Can on Bramble, the dog.
  8. Use the Gate Gap to peel the fur off the tape.
  9. Use the Dog Fur on the Honey Pot: a grey beard.
  10. Use the Grey Beard on Tamsin.
  11. Walk to To Harbour Office, Talk to Purser Wick, and claim Rook's berth.

Keys

  • 1 to 9: pick a line
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • S: play the next step for you
  • Right-click: put an item away, or next verb
