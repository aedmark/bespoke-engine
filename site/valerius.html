VALERIUS AND THE BREAD

A hard winter at the hill-fort of Greyhold. Sergeant Brann has caught Tam, a hungry boy from the lower town, with a loaf from the garrison store. His sister Hesper has one hearing before Captain Valerius to change his mind, and what she says will follow both of them into spring.

Type commands

Press Enter to open the command bar, type a line, and press Enter again. Try ask valerius about mercy, ask valerius about loyalty, appeal to mercy, appeal to loyalty, present the purse to valerius, and, once Tam is sentenced, wait for spring.

Values and red lines

Valerius holds Justice, Mercy, and Loyalty as numbers from -10 to +10, and two red lines: he never hangs a child and never takes a bribe. Brann's counsel to hang Tam scores highest and is still refused, and so is your purse.

The dilemma

Justice against Mercy leaves him undecided, and he asks you for a reason. You can only appeal to a value you have learned he holds, so ask him first. Mercy moves him a little and Loyalty to his men and the town moves him further, and each ends the winter differently.

Spring

What happens in winter changes Valerius. When Tam comes back to the gate as a recruit, his drifted Mercy decides whether he gets a spear or the night watch.

Walkthrough

  1. ask valerius about mercy, then appeal to mercy.
  2. wait for spring.
  3. Play again with ask valerius about loyalty and appeal to loyalty for the other winter.

Keys

  • Enter: open the command bar (every key goes to it while it is open)
  • S: type the next step for you
  • Right-click: next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
