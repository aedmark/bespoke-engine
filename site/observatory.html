THE LARKSPUR OBSERVATORY

1958, a mountain observatory, and the photographic plate that shows a new comet is gone. Ines Varga has until morning to find out who took it, while the staff go about their night and each of them has a reason to head her off.

Type commands

Press Enter to open the command bar, type a line, and press Enter again. Try go to the archive, pick up the exposure slip, read the telescope log, examine the telescope, or ask pell about the slip. Clicking a mark also walks Ines there, and clicking a person talks to them.

Agendas

Halloran, Pell, and Moss each pursue an agenda of their own and intercept Ines when her evidence threatens it. Pell plants an alibi if she reads the telescope log before she has the exposure slip in hand, so the order you gather evidence in matters. Nothing about that is scripted per encounter; the agendas choose from what the world holds.

Walkthrough

  1. go to the archive, then pick up the exposure slip.
  2. read the telescope log.
  3. Find Pell and put the slip to him. He is cornered and names Halloran.

Keys

  • Enter: open the command bar (every key goes to it while it is open)
  • S: type the next step for you
  • Right-click: next verb
  • I: state inspector (the facts as a graph; the game pauses)
