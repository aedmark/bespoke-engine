BRACKWATER BARBS

A duel of barbs and comebacks on the docks of Brackwater. Lose a round, learn a comeback, and work your way up to Mistress Vell.

Start a duel

With Talk selected, click a fencer on the Tidewalk: Bosun Pike, Old Gannet, or Dory Quill. Or press S to duel the next one in turn, ending with Mistress Vell in her fencing yard.

Trade barbs

  • Pick a line with the number keys or a click.
  • When a fencer insults you, answer with the right comeback to win the point.
  • On your turn, throw a barb. If they have no comeback, the point is yours.
  • First to three points wins.

Learn as you lose

You start knowing nothing. Every barb you hear is yours to throw, and every comeback a fencer uses on you is yours to keep. A joke always loses the round; on your turn it hands the fencer the next attack, so you hear a new barb. Each fencer knows different barbs and comebacks.

Keys

  • 1 to 9: pick a line
  • Enter, Space, or a click: next message
  • S: next duel
  • Right-click: next verb
