**What works**

- **Spatial Topology & Director:** Waypoint graphs, A* pathfinding, scene partitioning, multi-scene transitions (`transition to scene:`), and dynamic backdrops (P3, P12, D-005, D-014).
- **Dialogue & Epistemology:** Fact-guarded choice pruning, relational balloons, NPC belief evaluation (`Believes`, `Knows`, `Rejects`), and repetition mood fatigue (P13, D-015, D-017, D-025).
- **Party Coordination & Matter Conservation:** Multi-character party switching (`switch_actor`), party portrait HUD, and generalized atomic item transfer (`World.transfer_item`, `SpatialSystem.can_transfer_item_with_world`, `SpatialSystem.can_access_portal`) preserving conservation of matter and diegetic sender/recipient portal reachability (P21, D-025, D-026).
- **Branch Worlds & Vector Conduits:** `TemporalHypervisor` copy-on-write `fork_branch` and `collapse_branch` arenas, countdown tick budgets, the expiry fail-state, backward-chaining teleological Horn clause solver, hyper-temporal fibers, pre-cognitive vector conduits (`VectorConduit`), bitemporal transceivers, Novikov loop validation, and The Copper Weathercock vertical slice (`proofs/weathercock.zig`, `--weathercock`) with time-travel rewind (P31, D-027). Atomic temporal snapshots (`P31-04`, D-048) carry the branch world, countdown, collapse state, teleology, and dream residue through `host.Timeline` and `.sav` version 2. Temporal sessions route VM, dialogue, display, and actions to the live branch and rebind to primary after collapse (`P31-05`, D-049).
- **Musical Drafts (P22-01, D-029):** `draft Name(n1..n4) [reverse Inverse]` and `weave Draft on Target` compile to a draft table and the `weave` opcode; `VM.weave_notes` resolves played notes (forward or declared reverse) to `Weave(caster, draft, target)` handlers; in-script weaves queue in `VM.pending_weaves` until `dispatch_pending_weaves`; unmatched weaves record `WeaveFizzled`.
- **Distaff (P22-02, D-030):** `DistaffInput` phrase buffer with reach limit, `AudioEvent.play_note` (C4..B4) and weave stingers, `RenderFrame.distaff` staff HUD with clickable notes; letter keys C to B play notes while enabled.

- **MML Scores (P28-01, D-060):** `src/mml.zig` parses one melodic or percussion track of MML into integer-timed events (192 ticks per whole note), with ties, re-read `[ ]n` repeats, an `L` loop point, `v` volume, `@` envelope presets, and `k`/`s`/`h` drums. P28-02 (D-061) plays a four-part `MmlScore` set on `Music.score`: one integer clock shared by all parts, a whole-score loop (every looping part restarts at its `L` once all parts have ended), the `@` envelope table, and the grid voices.
