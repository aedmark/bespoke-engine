Array repetition (`**`) is gone (use `@splat`), `b.args` is `addPassthruArgs()`, `pathFromRoot` is `b.root.joinString`, and `@typeInfo` enums and structs list `field_names` and `field_values` / `field_types` (prefer `std.meta.tags` and `stringToEnum`). Native builds keep `use_llvm = true` (the self-hosted linker cannot link SDL2).
